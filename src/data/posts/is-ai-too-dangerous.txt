Is AI "Too Dangerous to Release" if we can't secure the release itself?

Anthropic called Claude Mythos too powerful for the public. It can find thousands of zero-day vulnerabilities in hours, which makes it a serious tool for defensive security.

> The real problem is not whether the model is powerful enough. It is whether the release process is secure enough.

## What the breach actually shows

The report that a Discord group gained access on day one through third-party credentials should make us uncomfortable for a very specific reason: it is not an AI failure, it is an operational failure.

## Why that matters

- We are rushing to ship increasingly capable systems while still struggling with basic credential management.
- Supply chain security remains a weak point even in organizations that care deeply about safety.
- A model can be technically impressive and still be wrapped in a fragile release process.

## The takeaway

Before we rely on AI to fix our code, we need to fix the environment it lives in. AI is a tool, not a magic shield, and this incident shows that even the most "secure" systems are still vulnerable to the human element.

Let’s stop treating AI as "perfect" and start treating it as what it is: a powerful, high-stakes technology that requires a security-first mindset.
