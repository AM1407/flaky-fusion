# 🏠 KotCompass — The Buggy Landlords

> **"The rescue for the 'slumlord' who still manages everything in Excel."**
> KotCompass is a central ecosystem that bridges the communication gap between students and landlords by replacing the chaos of Excel and WhatsApp with a streamlined management portal.

---

## 📖 About the Project
Managing student housing is often an administrative challenge. Contracts and agreements are scattered across various channels, and the status of maintenance is frequently unclear.

**KotCompass** offers an integrated solution:
1. **Search Platform:** An "AutoScout" experience for student housing with extensive filters and a responsive interface.
2. **Management Tool:** A portal where landlords manage their portfolio and maintain direct contact with their tenants via a **2-way communication panel**.

## 🚀 Key Features

### 💬 Direct Communication (2-Way Panel)
Instead of a rigid ticketing system, we implemented a **bi-directional messaging panel**. This allows tenants and landlords to:
* Directly report defects or ask questions.
* Centrally view the history of agreements without relying on external apps like WhatsApp.
* Quickly coordinate on maintenance issues within the context of the specific unit.

### 🏢 For the Landlord
* **Portfolio Overview:** Instant insight into occupancy rates and active properties.
* **Property Management:** A hierarchical structure (Building > Floor > Unit).
* **Tenant Management:** Link registered students to specific units with an overview of contract dates.

### 🎓 For the Student
* **My Kot:** A personal dashboard with all the details regarding their current rental situation.
* **Smart Search:** Advanced filters for price, location, contract duration, and amenities.
* **Digital Inspection:** A digital flow for the move-in inspection including photo uploads to prevent later disputes.
